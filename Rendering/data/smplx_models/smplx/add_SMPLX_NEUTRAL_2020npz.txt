SMPLX_NEUTRAL_2020.npz goes here.

You should not have to do this by hand: download.sh (step 3b) symlinks it to
3-Body/GVHMR/inputs/checkpoints/body_models/smplx/SMPLX_NEUTRAL.npz, which is
the same model. Run download.sh after the SMPLX registration step.

To do it manually instead, download the model from
https://smpl-x.is.tue.mpg.de/
and drop it here under the name SMPLX_NEUTRAL_2020.npz (the name is hardcoded
in Rendering/render_utils/*.py).
